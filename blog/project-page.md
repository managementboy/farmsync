Farm Sync is a small, free and open-source Android app that backs up your Stardew Valley farms and keeps them in sync between your Android devices through your own Google Drive. Version 1.0 came out on 8 November 2026.

- It shows every farm on the device with its in-game date and play time.
- It makes verified backups automatically, before every change, and restores them safely.
- It syncs each farm through a folder in your own Google Drive whenever you open the app. If a farm was played on two devices, the version with more play time continues and the other one is kept.
- It never writes while Stardew is open, and never overwrites unsaved mid-day progress without asking.
- It speaks English and Spanish.

To use it you need Android 13 or newer, Stardew Valley from Google Play, and the free helper app Shizuku. The [setup guide](https://github.com/managementboy/farmsync/blob/main/docs/SETUP.md) takes about 15 minutes per device and needs no computer. The [user guide](https://github.com/managementboy/farmsync/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.md) explains every screen. The app itself is on the [release page](https://github.com/managementboy/farmsync/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
